Event Information

Paper Presentation

Participants will present their research paper or innovative idea by explaining the title, abstract, problem statement, objectives, literature review, proposed methodology, results/findings, conclusion, and future scope before the judging panel.

The presentation should demonstrate significance, technical knowledge, originality and practical relevance. Teams will be evaluated based on research quality, technical content, innovation, clarity, communication skills and ability to answer questions.

Each team should consist of 2 to 3 members and complete their presentation within the allotted time.
